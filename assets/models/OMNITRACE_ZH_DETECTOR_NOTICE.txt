Calibration
-----------
The 0.98 AI-evidence gate was chosen on the combined development split — the
lowest candidate whose false-positive Wilson 95% upper bound stays under 1% —
and reported once on each of three corpora that took no part in training.
It is one-sided: crossing it supports AI authorship, but a score below it is an
abstention, never a vote for human authorship.

Fine-tuning, quantisation and calibration are OmniTrace work and are not
representations made by the base-model or corpus authors.

Reproduce with training/train_zh_detector.py and
training/export_hf_sequence_classifier.py; commands are in training/README.md.
