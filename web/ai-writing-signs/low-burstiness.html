Low Burstiness in AI Writing

Burstiness describes how much sentence length and rhythm vary. Some generated text feels unusually even: similar sentence lengths, regular paragraph transitions, and tidy explanations.

Why It Matters

Many language models produce smooth, stable prose. That can lower burstiness compared with a human draft that mixes short emphasis, long qualifications, fragments, and uneven revision marks.

But low burstiness is not proof. Formal academic writing, non-native writing, templates, abstracts, legal clauses, and heavily edited prose can all look regular. That is why a detector should combine burstiness with other evidence.

How to Interpret It

Read burstiness as a weak-to-moderate signal. It becomes more useful when it lines up with other patterns such as repeated transitions, low lexical diversity, citation anomalies, weak source history, and sentence-level model scores.
