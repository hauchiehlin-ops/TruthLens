Reference checks

Fake Citations as an AI Writing Signal

A polished paragraph can still cite papers that do not exist, mismatch authors and titles, or point to journals that never published the claimed work.

Why Language Scores Are Not Enough

AI systems can generate citations that look structurally correct. A reference may include a credible author, year, journal, volume, and DOI-like string while failing independent bibliographic checks.

That makes citation verification a separate evidence family. It should not be collapsed into a text AI score, because a human can also mistype citations and a model can cite real sources.

What to Verify

  • Whether the DOI exists and resolves to the claimed title.
  • Whether the author, year, journal, volume, and pages agree across public databases.
  • Whether multiple references cluster around vague or unverifiable claims.
  • Whether the suspicious claim is central to the paper's argument.
