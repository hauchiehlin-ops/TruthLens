DetectRL-ZH character n-gram detector notice

This bundled asset (detectrl_zh_char_svm.json) is trained by OmniTrace from two
public corpora. The model file contains only learned vectorizer terms, IDF
weights and linear coefficients — no corpus text is redistributed.

Training corpora
----------------
DetectRL-X
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/WUJUNCHAO/DetectRL-X
  License: MIT
  Generators: DeepSeek-V3, Gemini-2.5-Flash, GPT-4o, Qwen-Max

NLPCC 2025 Shared Task 1 (DetectRL-ZH)
  https://github.com/NLP2CT/NLPCC-2025-Task1
  Generators: GPT-4o, GLM-4-flash, Qwen-turbo

Calibration
-----------
The AI-evidence gate is the 99.5th percentile of human scores on the combined
development split. It is deliberately one-sided: crossing it supports AI
authorship, but a low score is an abstention, never a vote for human authorship.

Reproduce with training/export_detectrl_zh_char_svm.py; the exact command is in
training/README.md.
